The Studio

We don't just shoot weddings. We remember them for you.

Apna Digital Zone started in a small room in Vishnu Nagar with one camera and a stubborn belief — that a family in Arrah deserves the same quality of wedding film as anyone in Delhi or Mumbai. Six hundred and eighty-seven events later, that hasn't changed.

Today we run a full in-house team: candid photographers, cinematographers, licensed drone pilots, and album designers who colour-grade every single frame before it reaches you.

  • In-house editing — your files never leave our studio
  • Licensed drone pilots for open-air and rooftop venues
  • Teaser film delivered within 7 days of the wedding
  • Karizma photobooks and premium canvas printing on site
